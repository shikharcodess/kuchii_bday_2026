CUSTOM 3D CHARACTER MODEL INSTRUCTIONS
=======================================
You can drop a custom 3D model here to replace the default character!

Filename: character.glb (place directly in this folder: public/models/character.glb)

Recommended Free Sources:
1. Ready Player Me (https://readyplayer.me)
   - Upload a photo of her or customize hair, eyes, makeup, and clothes.
   - Download as .glb and name it character.glb!

2. Mixamo (https://www.mixamo.com)
   - Free rigged characters with walk, idle, and dance animations.

If no character.glb is provided, the website uses the built-in cute stylized character.
