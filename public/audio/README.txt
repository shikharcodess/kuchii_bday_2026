CUSTOM AUDIO FILES INSTRUCTIONS
===============================
You can drop your custom audio files (.mp3 or .wav) directly into this folder (public/audio/):

1. bgm.mp3
   - Ambient background music played throughout the walk.
   - Recommended: Soft acoustic guitar, piano, or warm lo-fi instrumental.

2. dance.mp3
   - Romantic slow-dance song played at the Finale stage when dancing with Shikhar!
   - Recommended: Your favorite song or romantic melody.

3. fire.mp3
   - Crackling fireplace ambience inside the cottage.

4. water.mp3
   - Gentle water ripples and bubbling near the Koi pond.

5. chime.mp3
   - Sound effect played when discovering surprises and interacting with stations.

If no custom files are provided, the site automatically uses built-in warm synthesized Web Audio chords!
